5 · Voice as a tool

A personal assistant with one extra tool: make_phone_call(phone_number, objective). The LLM decides when a call is the right move, places it, and the call transcript streams in live. Try: "Book a table for 4 at Luigi's this Friday around 7" or "Call the dentist and get me a cleaning next week".

Both sides of a simulated call are LLM agents voiced with different voices of the selected TTS. With Vocal Bridge, your Vocal Bridge agent places and holds the call (the objective is passed as session metadata) and the transcript is read back from its call logs. Twilio calls use Twilio's speech recognition + <Say> with our LLM deciding what to say; they need TWILIO_* and a public PUBLIC_URL (e.g. ngrok) in .env.
