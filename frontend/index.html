Voice Agent Lab

A local, provider-agnostic rebuild of DeepLearning.AI's Voice for AI Agents and Applications. Pick a stack in each page's settings bar: Open source (faster-whisper · Ollama · Kokoro, fully offline) or Google Gemini (Gemini STT · Gemini 3.8 Flash · Gemini TTS · Gemini Live). The Vocal Bridge page connects to the platform the course itself uses.

1 · The voice stack

Cascaded STT → LLM → TTS with per-stage latency. Mix and match providers and compare.

2 · Voice-to-voice

Speech-native realtime model over WebRTC — lower latency, less control. Side-by-side trade-offs.

3 · Voice in your app

Tic-tac-toe where voice commands and mouse clicks share one synchronized state.

4 · Voice for your agent

Wrap an existing RAG + tools text agent with voice — unchanged. Plus foreground/background dual-agent mode.

5 · Voice as a tool

An assistant with a make_phone_call tool. Simulated calls with live transcript, or real calls via Twilio.

Vocal Bridge (course platform)

The official SDK: client actions, AI Agent mode, outbound calls, and call evals.

6 · Voice AI evals

Simulated callers, audio round-trip WER, LLM-as-judge rubrics, latency, and regression tracking.
