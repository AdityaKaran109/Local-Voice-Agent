# Warranty

All Velo Bikes frames carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. Components (brakes, gears, wheels) are covered for 2 years. E-bike batteries and motors are covered for 2 years or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

The warranty does not cover crash damage, normal wear items (tires, brake pads, chains), or bikes used for commercial rental.

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID and photos of the issue. Approved claims are repaired or replaced free of charge, including shipping.
