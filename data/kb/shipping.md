# Shipping

Velo Bikes ships to the US and Canada. Standard shipping is free on orders over $100 and takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping costs $25 and takes 2 business days.

Bikes ship 90% assembled in a single box. Final assembly (handlebars, pedals, front wheel) takes about 20 minutes with the included tools.

Orders placed before 2pm Eastern ship the same business day. You receive a tracking link by email as soon as the label is created.
