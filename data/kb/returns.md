# Returns and refunds

You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Bikes must have fewer than 50 miles on them and be returned in the original box.

Return shipping is free for bikes. For accessories, a $7 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse. Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but may be eligible for warranty repair.

Velo Bikes does not issue discount codes through customer support. Promotions are only announced through the newsletter.
